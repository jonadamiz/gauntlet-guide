Gauntlet of Pirates · map guide

The 5v5 map, west to east

Both sides start in the west and push east through 6 regions, each one harder than the last. The map below is the one the game draws; everything on it is placed where the game places it.

Drag to move, scroll or use + and − to zoom, hover a marker to name it. The pills show or hide each kind.

How the map is crossed

  • Both sides start in the west, Navy at Thundoria Castle and Pirate at Shaitan City, and fight east along one road.
  • Each region past the start is shrouded in poison fog until the boss of the region before it falls in that match; the fog takes 10% of your maximum HP every second. One kill lifts it for both sides.
  • On foot, only the fog closes a region; there is no level gate. A Gatekeeper teleports you back to your base, or to any region whose level you have reached and whose fog has lifted.
  • The Black Dragon Lair is reached only through the Lair Warden, by a player of Lv 75 or more who carries the Black Dragon Key. Killing Icy Dragon or Thief - Wang Xiao Hu grants the key, once per player, to the side that did the most damage.
  • Killing the Black Dragon wins the match for the side of the player who dealt it the most damage (damage, not threat: taunting it does not take the win).
  • Die in an open region and you come back at your side's spawn point there; die in the fog and you come back in the nearest open region to the west.
  • There are no safe zones on this map: nowhere disables fighting.
