Ship’s Log patch notes

Oct

Gauntlet of Pirates 21 - 24 Oct

  • The game is now Gauntlet of Pirates, with new loading-screen tips.
  • Gear grows with your character; the Lv1-65 upgrade runestones are retired.
  • Pearls, a Grants tab, gem-slot colours and team colours.
  • Targeting: a range ring, no aiming while a skill cools down, no skills on NPCs, and Ctrl picks enemies only.
  • Debuff icons, a Deposit to Vault menu, the area name on the minimap, and Healing Spring on allies.
  • The camera catches up when it falls behind, and the game installs with nothing extra to download.
Oct

Class skill bars and a new in-game UI 16 - 22 Oct

  • Per-class skill bars replace loadouts, and save themselves.
  • HUD and window scaling, a status bar, and a reworked inventory and guild vault.
  • Champion select gets an apparel pane that dresses your champion, plus a light theme and a colourblind mode.
  • Lobby wallets, claimed dailies, party flow, an apparel grid and match history.
  • A Gem and Fruit Exchanger, a kill bounty, coloured notices and more Gatekeepers.
Oct

The 5v5 map 7 - 15 Oct

  • Silver Mine is cut; its bridge now joins Forsaken City.
  • Shared rations, fairy and set swaps, and Chaos gear that levels with you.
  • A runestone path from a Lv1 starting kit, with gear you cannot lose.
  • Skill tuning, neutral mini bosses, and changes to revival and respawn.
  • In-match chat and a team quest counter.
  • The Black Dragon can no longer heal itself.
  • A Market dressing room, player profiles, and Rankings with Bounty.
Oct

Abaddon cut, the Market opens 1 - 3 Oct

  • Abaddon is cut; the isthmus now leads onto the Black Dragon's Nest.
  • New Cardic weapon stats.
  • The lobby Market, guilds and a cosmetics wardrobe.
  • Every Demonic Fruit says what its fairy adds; Om gives 20% max SP.
Sep

Runestone crowns and magic resist 23 - 30 Sep

  • Magic Resist is its own stat, and it now reaches damage.
  • Level difference no longer changes damage.
  • Bosses are 30% weaker, crowd control is shorter, and ores pay Refining Gems.
  • The Chaos Crownstone climbs to Lv75 as one of four runestone crowns; crowns give 500 SP and 750 HP.
  • The Chaotic Runestone works on Lv65 gear, and respawn at Lv75 takes 38 seconds.
  • Pet fruit evolution: nine fruits, nine pet effects.
  • Base move speed 520, reworked newbie chests, bulk buying, and skills you can cancel.
Sep

The clash density rework 13 - 20 Sep

  • Lone Tower and Black Dragon Lair 1 are cut, and the level cap is now 75.
  • A runestone chain, side grants, poison fog, the Black Dragon Key, PvP stakes and boss notices.
  • Each side spawns at its own base with its own Gatekeepers; a Weightless Potion at every rung.
  • Chests refill every 15 minutes.
  • An I'm Stuck button in the ESC menu.
  • Characters no longer turn invisible on AMD graphics.
  • Server hardening: one bad packet can no longer take the server down.
Sep

5v5 test rounds 5 - 12 Sep

  • Gem slots, caps and descriptions, match length, and monster crowd-control lockouts.
  • Fairies feed themselves from rations, and eating a food or potion again adds to its time.
  • Forge the gear you are wearing; socket level follows player level.
  • Rebirth into the same class keeps your build.
  • Remappable hotbar keys, hold Space to buff yourself, and players-only targeting.
  • Gear from chests, consumables from quests, and an unseal system that works.
