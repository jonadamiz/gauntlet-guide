Gauntlet: Dragon Risen

Every level, class by class

Base attributes do not rise on their own — a level grants points, and the attributes move only when you spend one. So every row below sits at the starting 5 in all six, and the numbers are the LEVEL's own contribution.

Only four stats carry a level term at all: Max HP, Max SP, Hit and Dodge. Attack, defence, crit, magic find, regen and attack speed have no Lv() in their formulas — they move only when an attribute or your gear does, which is why each class states them once rather than repeating one number down forty rows. First classes run 1–40, second classes 41–85. All 10 classes, produced by running the game's own ExAttrCheck once per level.

How a base attribute turns into a stat

Every one of these numbers comes out of a single line (AttrCalculate.lua:1759 for attack, and the same shape for the rest). It has two parts, and reading them as one is what makes the table look random:

1 · the steady part
Strength × 1.5

A flat rate per point. The catch: it is often a fraction, and the game floors the result — so a ×1.5 rate cannot pay out 1.5 each time. It pays +1, +2, +1, +2. That alternation is not a bonus and not a pattern worth planning around; it is one rate landing either side of a whole number.

2 · the milestone part
0.4 × floor(Strength × 4 ÷ 20)2

This sits still, then steps every time the floor ticks over — every 5 points for attack, every ~7 for HP, SP and defence. These are the real bonuses. And because the term is squared, each milestone is worth more than the last: attributes get better the more you invest, not worse.
