Gauntlet of Pirates · boss & loot guide

Everything you can kill or open.

Every boss, mini boss, chest and monster the 5v5 map places, region by region: what it drops, how likely each drop is, and what killing it pays.

  • Ordinary monsters carry no drop table; they pay gold. A monster of Lv 35 or below pays 45; any other pays by where its killer stands: 135 in Forsaken City, 165 in Dark Swamp, 195 in Demonic World, 285 in Black Dragon's Nest. Every kill also shares an Auto Ration and a chance at a fairy stat fruit with the side.
  • A boss's drop table falls only on its first kill in a match. Later kills drop nothing, but still pay the bounty and grants on its card.
  • Chests draw their vouchers from a marble bag per side and match, so those rates are exact over a full bag rather than independent rolls. Their runestones (Demonic World's chests) roll on their own, and the opener's drop-rate bonus adds to them.
