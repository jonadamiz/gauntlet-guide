Gauntlet: Dragon Risen · boss & loot guide

Everything you can kill or open.

Every boss, mini boss and chest on the Gauntlet, what it drops, and how likely each drop is. Use the pills to narrow by kind, or search a name, a region or an item.

Ordinary monsters pay gold, not items. All 90 of them carry no drop table — but every kill pays gold, and the rate rises as you push east: 45 around the starting towns, then 105 in Silver Mine through to 285 in the Black Dragon Lair. Items come from bosses, minis and chests; gold comes from everything.
